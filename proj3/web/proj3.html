Part B.4: RANSAC for Robust Homography

For RANSAC, I had difficulty calculating a correct N to iterate through given I couldn't solve what percentage of my correspondences were actually correct, so I arbitrarily set N=1000. This was also a bit challenging as the outputs to my previous functions were a bit confusing... so a good lesson in software engineering for me. Overall, I'm really glad with the result, and was able to autostitch a plethora of photos I took in the beginning of 3A that would take too long to manually stitch! I was also able to merge two timepoints of photos, one from golden hour and one from a foggy daytime photo. The images are a bit blurry likely due to the low quality of the images and poor blending which could be improved in the future.
